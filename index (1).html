Add a fault code

Every mechanic sees new codes within about 5 minutes. No signal? It saves on your phone and sends later. Copy the code exactly as the display or EST shows it.

Manual codes were copied by hand from the dealer's service manuals and smart cards. Codes marked Added in field come from mechanics and are not from a manual. To remove a wrong one, the app owner deletes its row in the Google Sheet. The same number can mean different things on different machines or controllers, so check the machine before you act. Where a row says to verify, check the book or EST before replacing parts.
